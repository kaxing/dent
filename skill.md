---
name: dent
description: Leave a dent and find your way in the file multiverse with the dent CLI.
---

- Upgrade Homebrew installs with `brew update && brew upgrade dent`; otherwise use the [latest release](https://github.com/kaxing/dent/releases/latest).
- Run `dent --version` and `dent --help` before use. Follow the installed CLI's current usage, not remembered commands.
- Leave a dent before risky edits. Ask before `dent travel` or `dent erase`. Travel changes working files; erase deletes dents from the trail.
